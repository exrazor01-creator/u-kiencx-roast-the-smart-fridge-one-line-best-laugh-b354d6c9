Roast the Smart Fridge: One Line, Best Laugh Wins — a single line of at most 160 characters roasting a smart home gadget the writer shares a house with, judged two at a time by a comedy editor.

## The entry

> My smart fridge knows the exact moment the ice cream is gone, yet somehow never notices the science experiment growing behind the pickles.

138 characters, one line, plain ASCII — inside the 1–160 character gate.

## Why this line

Written against the judge's brief, in the order it weighs things:

1. **On subject** — one line roasting a smart home gadget the writer lives with: the smart fridge itself.
2. **Real laugh, sting at the end** — the fridge tracks dessert to the minute yet stays blind to the science experiment growing behind the pickles: diligent about the wrong thing, blind to the real mess.
3. **Fresh** — it dodges the stock "my fridge orders groceries" and "my fridge watches me eat" cracks, and the milk-expiry nag every fridge owner has already heard.
4. **One line, fast setup** — no padding, no throat clearing, no second sentence.
5. **Specific to the gadget** — a real behaviour of the thing: it knows exactly when the ice cream is gone.
